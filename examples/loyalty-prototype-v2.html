Dog icon: profile menu. “Log in” signs you in as a member; “Not a member” shows the join step. “View activity” opens the history.

What changed in v2

Applied from the Yotpo review

  • New state: logged in but not a member yet (“You’re a step away from rewards”, optional terms checkbox, join button).
  • Points card now matches Yotpo: “Hi Ben!”, the point balance and two buttons. The “Worth $12.50” line and the progress bar are gone.
  • Recent activity list replaced by a “View activity” button that opens the history dialog (Date, Action, Points, Status, Expires).
  • “Your rewards” is now “Ways to redeem”: read-only list, no per-customer status. Redeeming happens at checkout.
  • Referral link is hidden: only the “Copy my referral link” button.
  • Copy: step 1 is “Join the club and earn 100 points”, and the “Join” tile now says 100 points too (the demo showed 100 and 50 for the same action).

Follow-ups

  • The “Worth $12.50” line and a progress bar can be built in the theme later, once the program is live to test.
  • Tiles and rewards are the Yotpo demo values; the real list comes from the rules Kismet configures. Clicking a tile (logged in) opens Yotpo modals that can be restyled, not redesigned.
  • If redeeming moves onto the page: Redeem, confirmation, generated code, points deducted at once. Needs its own screens.
